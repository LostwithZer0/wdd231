Minecraft Survival Guide

Individual Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

Minecraft Survival Guide

This name was selected because it clearly describes a website dedicated to helping players learn and progress through Minecraft survival mode.

Site Purpose

The website provides survival tips and progression guides for Minecraft players. Visitors can learn about gathering resources, crafting equipment, building shelters, and exploring the world. The website will also provide a feedback form for visitors to submit suggestions.

Scenarios

  • What should I do during my first day in Minecraft survival mode?
  • What resources and equipment do I need to progress through the game?
  • How can I prepare for exploring the Nether and defeating the Ender Dragon?

Color Scheme

  • Forest Green (#243524) - Header, headings, navigation, and footer.
  • Warm Off-White (#F4F1E8) - Page background.
  • White (#FFFFFF) - Content backgrounds.
  • Charcoal (#252525) - Body text.
  • Grass Green (#477A38) - Buttons and accents.

Typography

Arial Bold - Used for headings.

Arial Regular - Used for body text and navigation.
