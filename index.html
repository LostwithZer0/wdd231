About Me

Hello! I'm David Hostetter, a software development student studying through BYU-Pathway Worldwide. I've always enjoyed technology, problem-solving, and finding creative ways to turn ideas into something functional. I'm especially interested in web development because it allows me to combine technical skills with creativity and design. Outside of school, I help run a small family business and enjoy working on creative projects, including website design and development. I'm also an avid gamer who loves Minecraft and other games that encourage creativity, exploration, and building. Most importantly, I'm a husband and proud father, and spending time with my family is one of my greatest priorities. My goal is to continue developing my programming skills, build a career in software development, and create websites and applications that are both useful and enjoyable to use.
